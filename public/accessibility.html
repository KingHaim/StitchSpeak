Accessibility

StitchSpeak is operated by Innovai Studio S.L. and aims to provide a usable experience for keyboard, screen reader, and mobile users.

Current Commitments

The app uses semantic HTML where possible, labeled controls, keyboard-accessible dialogs, and responsive layouts.

Known Work

We continue to improve contrast, focus states, exported document accessibility, and translated pattern rendering across devices.

Feedback

If you find an accessibility issue, contact support@stitchspeak.com with the page, device, browser, and what happened.
