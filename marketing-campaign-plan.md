# StitchSpeak Marketing Campaign Plan

## Campaign Thesis

Campaign name: **Your patterns, ready for more markets**

Primary objective: turn independent knitwear and crochet designers into beta applicants, activated users, and public proof points.

Primary CTA: **Apply for free beta access**

Secondary CTA: **Start translating**

Positioning guardrail: StitchSpeak is self-service pattern-writing and translation software. It is not a bespoke translation, technical editing, or pattern-design service. Every campaign asset should say or imply: upload, estimate, translate, review, export. Never imply that StitchSpeak replaces designer judgment before publication.

## Market Snapshot

- Ravelry is a major gathering place for knitters, crocheters, designers, yarns, patterns, projects, and groups: https://www.ravelry.com/about
- Ravelry reported nearly 1.2M patterns in its database after 87,106 patterns were added in 2022: https://blog.ravelry.com/2022-community-stats/
- Etsy explicitly shows knit and crochet sellers using instantly downloadable PDF pattern lines, which validates the digital pattern seller segment: https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/26915505154
- Pinterest positions itself as a place where people discover, plan, and shop, with ads across awareness, consideration, leads, and sales: https://business.pinterest.com/en-gb/
- Competing alternatives are often professional or human translation services, e.g. Knitlingo and individual specialist translators. StitchSpeak should differentiate on self-service speed, transparent credits, and a reusable workspace: https://www.knitlingo.com/ and https://www.mediaperuana.com/translation

## Ideal Customer

Primary segment: independent knitwear and crochet designers who sell original PDF patterns through Ravelry, Etsy, Shopify, Gumroad, Payhip, or their own site.

Best-fit profile:

- Has at least 10 published patterns or one strong-selling pattern.
- Has an audience, usually Instagram, newsletter, Ravelry group, YouTube, or Pinterest.
- Wants to reach knitters outside their home language but is not ready for slow, expensive manual translation.
- Is comfortable reviewing pattern copy before publishing.
- Values accuracy around rows, repeats, abbreviations, sizing, measurements, and pattern terminology.

PVP score:

| Segment | Personal fit | Market value | Profitability | Total |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Independent designers with existing audience | 9 | 9 | 8 | 26/30 |
| Small yarn brands and publishers | 7 | 8 | 8 | 23/30 |
| Technical editors and pattern translators as partners | 8 | 7 | 7 | 22/30 |
| Hobby knitters translating patterns for personal use | 7 | 5 | 4 | 16/30 |

Recommended focus: start with independent designers. They have direct pain, public audiences, and can create proof for the next segment.

## USP And Message

Only statement:

> StitchSpeak is the self-service translation workspace for independent knitwear and crochet designers who want to upload one pattern, see the cost first, translate into 13 languages, review pattern-native terminology, and export copy for their own layout.

Elevator pitch:

> You know how independent pattern designers want to sell beyond one language, but manual translation feels slow, expensive, and hard to coordinate? StitchSpeak lets them upload a pattern, approve the credit estimate, translate with knitting-aware terminology, and review the copy in one private workspace so their next release can reach more markets.

Main promise:

**Translate your best pattern into a new market before your next launch.**

Support claims:

- Pattern language, not generic prose.
- One upload, 13 language options.
- Cost shown before credits are used.
- Translations saved in a private pattern library.
- Exportable copy for the designer's own layout.
- Credits never expire.

Avoid these claims:

- "Professionally translated for you."
- "Ready to publish without proofreading."
- "We preserve your final designed PDF layout."
- "We design or edit your patterns."

## Core Offer

Beta offer:

**Free designer beta access for selected independent designers in exchange for testing StitchSpeak on a real pattern and sharing an honest experience with their audience.**

Offer page promise:

> Apply with one pattern, one target market, and one way you would show your audience the translation process.

Who qualifies:

- Owns the rights to the pattern being translated.
- Has a clear release or re-release plan.
- Can review the translated output before publishing.
- Can share one honest public post, Reel, Story, newsletter mention, or Ravelry/community update.

Paid fallback:

Designers who do not join beta can buy credits starting at EUR 7.00, with a standard pattern often starting around 6.5 credits, subject to the app's estimate.

## Funnel

1. Cold attention: Instagram/Reels, Pinterest pins, creator outreach, partner mentions.
2. Landing page: beta campaign page with short demos and application form.
3. Lead capture: beta application plus optional waitlist for designers not accepted.
4. Nurture: 6-email sequence explaining workflow, safety, cost, use cases, and publication review.
5. Conversion: approve beta applicants or route to standard credit purchase.
6. Activation: first upload, first estimate, first translation, first export.
7. Advocacy: ask activated beta users to share their workflow and refer one designer friend.

## Channel Strategy

Primary channel: **Instagram/Reels + direct creator outreach**

Why: the beta form already asks for Instagram handle and audience size, demos are visual, and designers can show workflow/results publicly.

Secondary channel: **Pinterest organic + small paid tests**

Why: patterns, craft workflows, and product discovery fit visual search behavior. Use pins that drive to beta or lead magnet pages.

Partnership channel: **technical editors, pattern-design educators, yarn shops, craft newsletters, and designer communities**

Why: these partners already serve designers and can lend trust.

Community channel: **Ravelry and niche groups**

Use carefully. Participate as a helpful resource, not as a spammy advertiser. Point to education, beta criteria, and transparent workflow.

## 30-Day Launch Plan

### Week 1: Setup

- Add UTM links for Instagram bio, Instagram DMs, Pinterest, partner outreach, and beta page.
- Create the Market-Ready Pattern Checklist.
- Prepare 3 demo clips from existing videos.
- Build a simple CRM sheet with source, handle, audience size, status, first upload, first translation, shared proof.
- Shortlist 50 designers and 20 potential partners.

### Week 2: Organic And Outreach

- Publish 3 Reels using the demo clips.
- Publish 5 Pinterest pins linking to beta.
- Send 50 personalized designer DMs.
- Send 10 partner outreach emails.
- Approve the first 5-10 beta testers.

### Week 3: Paid Test

- Run a small Instagram/Reels test with 2 hooks and 2 creatives.
- Run a small Pinterest test with 3 pin variants.
- Retarget beta page visitors with the "Watch it work" angle.
- Pause anything with poor click-through or low-quality applications.

### Week 4: Proof And Referral

- Turn the first successful beta user into a mini case study.
- Ask every activated beta user for one referral.
- Publish "what we learned from the first beta translations."
- Review CAC, application quality, activation, and shared proof.

## Metrics

Track weekly:

- Landing visits by channel.
- Visitor to beta application conversion.
- Cost per qualified application.
- Approval rate.
- Approved applicant to first upload.
- First upload to first completed translation.
- Completed translation to public share.
- Referral invites sent.
- New leads from referrals.
- Paid users or credit purchases from non-beta leads.

Starting targets:

- Beta landing conversion: 10-20%.
- Qualified application cost: under EUR 15 until LTV is known.
- Approved applicant activation: 60%+ first upload within 7 days.
- Activated beta public proof: 30%+ share within 14 days.
- Referral: 1 referred designer per 3 activated beta users.

## Referral Program

Trigger: after a designer completes their first translation or posts publicly about the beta.

Mechanism: unique referral link or "who referred you?" form field.

Incentive:

- Referrer gets bonus beta credits or priority access to new features.
- Referred designer gets priority beta review or starter credits if not selected.

Referral ask:

> I am glad you got your first translation through StitchSpeak. Do you know one designer friend who is sitting on a best-selling pattern that could work in another language? If yes, you can send them this beta link and mention your name.

## One-Page Marketing Score

Current score: **7/10**

What is strong:

- Clear niche.
- Clear product promise.
- Existing beta page and application workflow.
- Visual demo assets already exist.
- Differentiation from manual service alternatives.

What is missing to reach 10/10:

- 5-10 real customer interviews with independent designers.
- Actual CAC and LTV by channel.
- A tested lead magnet landing page.
- Automated email nurture implementation.
- Public proof from beta users.
- Referral tracking inside the app or beta form.

Highest-impact next step:

Run the 30-day beta campaign with one primary channel, Instagram/Reels + outreach, and one secondary channel, Pinterest. Do not scale paid spend until beta application quality and first-translation activation are measurable.
